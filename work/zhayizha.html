Zhayizha扎一扎

From saved travel posts to places in your own Google Maps.把想去的地方，扎进地图。

Built with Codex与 Codex 协作开发 Local trial本机试用中 2026.10

I kept saving travel posts on Xiaohongshu, then looking up the same places again before a trip. Zhayizha reads the text and images, extracts place candidates, and saves confirmed locations to my own Google Maps list. Each save is checked in Maps before it is marked complete.

I defined the use case and acceptance criteria, supplied real travel posts, and iterated with Codex on implementation and testing. As the prototype grew, I narrowed the app to account connections, source and destination settings, and necessary reviews. The places belong in the map people already use.

The workflow works locally. New locations still need confirmation, and a ready-to-install public app is not yet available. The next steps are more reliable background processing and fewer manual checks.

旅行攻略收藏了很多，出发前却还要逐个查店名、辨认分店、再存一遍地图。扎一扎读取小红书攻略的正文与图片，提取地点候选；确认具体位置后，保存到自己的 Google Maps 列表，并在地图中复核结果。

我负责定义需求、提供真实攻略和验收反馈，与 Codex 协作完成实现与测试。迭代后，我把 App 收缩到账号连接、来源与目标设置、必要核对，让地点留在用户已经习惯使用的地图里。

目前已跑通本机使用流程，新地点仍需确认，尚未提供面向大众的即用安装版。接下来重点完善后台处理的可靠性，减少逐项操作。
