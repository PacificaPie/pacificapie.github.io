Beyond the Heatmap看懂热图之后

If an explanation looks right, does the model understand?解释看起来合理，就说明模型理解了吗？

A heatmap can make an answer feel intuitive. I am more interested in the next step: what happens when the information it highlights is changed or removed?一张热图很容易让回答显得直观。我更关心接下来的一步：改变或删掉它标出的信息，模型的行为真的会跟着变化吗？

Evidence checkpoint证据检查点 · 2026-10-20

The question关注的问题

Use explanations to form a testable hypothesis. Compare attribution with behavior after an intervention, rather than treating a plausible picture as the conclusion.让解释帮助提出可验证的假设，再把归因与干预后的行为对照，而不是把一张合理的图当成结论。

What the notes contain目前积累了什么

Coursework includes attribution comparisons on limited samples. Multimodal classroom analysis also surfaced repeated “no” answers—a collapse worth diagnosing rather than reading as understanding.课程作业包含有限样本上的归因方法比较。多模态课堂分析也出现了持续回答 no 的塌缩现象；这更值得诊断，不能直接解释成模型理解。

How I read the evidence怎样看待证据

Keep sample scope, failure cases, and alternative explanations visible. The cover is a conceptual illustration, not an experimental heatmap or a performance chart.保留样本范围、失败案例和其他可能解释。封面是机制插画，不是实验热图，也不承载性能或准确率结论。

The next useful test下一步，验证什么

Turn selected assignments into focused notes: one question, one comparison, and the limits of what it establishes. Use these checks to inform product evaluation, not to overstate model understanding.下一步把精选作业整理成短笔记：一个问题、一次对照，以及它能说明和不能说明什么。让这些检查服务产品评测，而不是夸大模型理解。
