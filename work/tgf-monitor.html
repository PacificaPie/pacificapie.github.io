TGF MonitorTGF Monitor

When an agent says “done,” what evidence should we ask for?Agent 说“完成了”，我们该追问什么证据？

An execution log tells us what happened. A useful monitor should help us ask the next question—and bring the answer back to a specific piece of evidence.执行日志能记录发生了什么。真正有用的监控，还应该帮助人继续追问，并把回答落回一条可核查的证据。

Evidence checkpoint证据检查点 · 2026-10-21

The product question产品问题

Make an agent’s work inspectable without asking a person to read every event. The interaction moves from a trace, to a targeted question, to an evidence location.让人不必逐条读完日志，也能检查 Agent 的工作：从执行轨迹出发，提出有针对性的追问，再回到证据所在。

What we have目前做到哪

The team has an implementation and development-set replays. Evaluation currently uses the same 64 development records and provisional, non-blind AI labels. These are development signals, not an independent advantage claim.团队已形成实现与开发集回放。当前评测仍使用同一批 64 条开发记录，标签来自暂定的 AI 非盲审核；这些是开发信号，还不能证明独立评测优势。

What I focus on我关注的判断

My focus is execution contracts, source checks, the distinction between public and private evidence, and evaluation endpoints and label audits. This is team work; the method and replay work are not all mine.我关注执行约定与来源核查、公开与私有证据的区分，以及评测端点和标签审计。这是团队研究，方法实现与回放工作不全部归于我。

The next useful test下一步，验证什么

Compare against baselines under matched cost and question budgets, then test whether the conclusions survive a more independent evaluation. A more persuasive answer is not automatically better evidence.下一步在匹配成本和问题数的条件下与基线比较，再检查更独立的评测是否支持结论。回答更有说服力，不自动等于证据更可靠。
