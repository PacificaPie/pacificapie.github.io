Subliminal LearningSubliminal Learning

Can seemingly unrelated training data carry a preference?看起来无关的训练数据，会悄悄传递偏好吗？

The project asks whether a model’s behavior can shift through data that does not explicitly describe that behavior. The first job is to reproduce the effect before proposing a defense.这个项目关心：数据没有直接说出某种偏好，模型的行为是否仍可能随训练改变？第一步是把现象复现清楚，再谈如何防御。

Evidence checkpoint证据检查点 · 2026-10-20

Why it matters为什么值得研究

A clean-looking dataset is not enough to establish how a trained model will behave. The useful question is what behavior changes, under which conditions, and whether that change persists.数据表面看起来干净，不足以说明训练后的模型会怎样行动。更值得追问的是：什么行为变了，在什么条件下变了，换个条件是否还成立？

What we have目前做到哪

The team has completed cat-versus-neutral replication with three seeds under a fixed model and configuration. Variation across seeds is substantial; the result should be read within those conditions.团队已在固定模型与配置下，完成 cat / neutral 的三种子复现。不同种子间差异明显，因此目前结果应限定在这组条件内理解。

The research discipline研究中的取舍

Keep replication, robustness, and defense claims separate. A result from one setting is a starting point for a test plan—not a general safety guarantee. This page summarizes a team course project.把复现、稳健性与防御结论分开。一组条件下的结果，是下一轮验证的起点，不能直接当成普遍的安全结论。本页是团队课程项目的阶段摘要。

The next useful test下一步，验证什么

Vary the relevant conditions and test candidate defenses against explicit baselines. New robustness and defense experiments had not run at the checkpoint above; no defense effectiveness is claimed here.下一步改变关键条件，并让候选防御接受明确基线的检验。截至上述检查点，新稳健性与防御实验尚未运行；目前不宣称防御有效。
